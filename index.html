2025 to now

Evolutionary alpha search on markets

An LLM proposes and mutates factor expressions for Russian equities, generation after generation. Every candidate is scored by walk-forward backtests over a decade of daily data and screened against multiple testing with the deflated Sharpe ratio, probability of backtest overfitting and the model confidence set, so that survivors are not artefacts of the search itself.

genetic programmingwalk-forward validationmultiple-testing control
